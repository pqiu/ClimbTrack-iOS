ClimbTrack

Privacy policy

ClimbTrack processes camera, detection, tracking, recording, and Bluetooth information on your iPhone. The app does not send this information to the developer or to an analytics service.

Effective November 12, 2026

Information collection

The ClimbTrack app does not collect personal data, create user accounts, display advertising, or include analytics, crash-reporting, or remote-inference services.

On-device processing

  • Camera frames are processed on the device for preview, person detection, gesture recognition, tracking, digital zoom, and recording.
  • YOLOX and Apple Vision inference run on the device.
  • Camera frames, recordings, detections, model output, and Bluetooth activity are not uploaded by the app.

Information stored on your device

  • The selected controller identifier and name, along with app settings, are stored locally in iOS user preferences.
  • Temporary recording files are kept in the app’s private storage while a video is being prepared.
  • Completed recordings are saved to your Photos library. You control those recordings using the Photos app.

Deleting ClimbTrack removes its locally stored preferences and private app data. Recordings already saved to Photos remain until you delete them from Photos.

Permissions

  • Camera: live preview, detection, tracking, and video recording.
  • Microphone: optional audio recorded with video.
  • Bluetooth: discovery and control of a compatible motor controller.
  • Add to Photos: saving completed recordings.

You can review or revoke permissions in the iPhone Settings app. Some features will not work when their required permission is disabled.

Network access and external links

ClimbTrack does not use a network connection to operate its camera, detection, tracking, recording, or motor-control features. Links in the app open documentation and project pages in your browser; those websites have their own privacy practices.

Children’s privacy

ClimbTrack is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Policy changes

If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the ClimbTrack support page.
