ClimbTrack

Safety guidance

ClimbTrack is a recreational filming tool, not a safety system. Detection, tracking, recording, Bluetooth communication, and motor commands can be delayed, interrupted, or incorrect.

Never rely on ClimbTrack for climbing safety

Do not use ClimbTrack for fall protection, belaying, rescue, access control, medical decisions, or any other safety-critical purpose. A person may be missed, confused with someone else, or lost during tracking.

Before using a motorized mount

  • Secure the phone, mount, tripod, cables, and controller.
  • Keep the mount away from climbing routes, fall zones, walkways, ropes, and protective equipment.
  • Keep people, hair, loose clothing, and equipment clear of moving parts and pinch points.
  • Test pan and tilt directions at low speed before normal use.
  • Confirm that motor speed limits are appropriate for the mount and surroundings.
  • Provide an accessible physical power switch or disconnect that stops the motors independently of the app.

During use

  • Supervise the device whenever motor power is enabled.
  • Do not leave the mount operating unattended.
  • Maintain enough clearance for the full pan and tilt range.
  • Stop using the system if the mount shakes, binds, overheats, disconnects repeatedly, or moves unpredictably.
  • Do not troubleshoot moving hardware near people or valuable equipment.

If the mount moves unexpectedly

  1. Stay clear of the mechanism.
  2. Use the physical power cutoff or disconnect motor power.
  3. Do not reconnect power until the cause is understood and the area is clear.
  4. Retest at low speed in an isolated area.

Report a safety issue

For a motor-control or security issue that could put people or property at immediate risk, use the repository’s private security reporting page. For non-sensitive problems, use the support page.
