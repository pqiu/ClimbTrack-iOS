Support

Get help with the ClimbTrack iPhone app, report a problem, or suggest an improvement.

Contact

ClimbTrack is developed by Peng Qiu. For app issues, general feedback, or feature requests, open a support request on GitHub. You can also contact the developer through the public contact method on the developer’s GitHub profile.

When reporting a problem, include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, selected detection model, whether a motor controller was connected, and the steps that reproduce the issue. Do not attach private recordings unless they are necessary and you are comfortable sharing them.

Camera or microphone access

Open iPhone Settings, locate ClimbTrack, and confirm that Camera and Microphone access are enabled. Microphone access is only needed when you want audio in a recording.

Videos are not appearing in Photos

Confirm that ClimbTrack has permission to add to Photos. Keep the app open while it finishes saving the recording.

The controller does not connect

Confirm that Bluetooth is enabled, the controller is powered, and it is near the iPhone. In ClimbTrack, open the device screen, disconnect any stale connection, and scan again.

Tracking or framing is unreliable

Use even lighting, keep the person visible, and avoid severe obstruction by other people or objects. Try another detection model from Advanced Settings if needed.

Security or immediate motor-safety reports

Do not post sensitive vulnerabilities or hazards that could create immediate physical risk in a public issue. Use the repository’s private security reporting page. Review the safety guidance before operating a motorized mount.
